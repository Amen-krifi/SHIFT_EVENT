Event Brief · SHIFT 2026

Where Business
Meets AI.

SHIFT — Where Business Meets AI is a one-day business-focused event organized by AIESEC in Bardo, bringing together business leaders, entrepreneurs, AI innovators, students, and young professionals to explore how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the way businesses operate, innovate, and create value.

Rather than focusing on the technical side of AI, SHIFT focuses on real-world applications, business opportunities, challenges, and the future of work through inspiring talks, expert discussions, interactive sessions, partner showcases, and a dedicated networking space. The event aims to create meaningful connections between the next generation of business builders and the people already shaping the future of business with AI.
